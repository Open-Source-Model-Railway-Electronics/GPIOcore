Setting up a servo

A servo turns to an angle between 0° and 180°. As a turnout motor it moves between two positions:

  • Position 0 (curved) — the angle for “curved”.
  • Position 1 (straight) — the angle for “straight”.
  • Speed — milliseconds per degree. 20 ms/° is calm and realistic; 5 is fast; 50 is very slow. A full movement takes |position 1 − position 0| × speed.

Finding the right positions — use “Live”

  1. Connect the board and tick Live.
  2. Drag the Position 0 slider slowly. The servo on the layout moves along with it. Stop when the turnout blade just touches the rail.
  3. Do the same for Position 1.
  4. Click Write to store it.

Tip: do not push the blade too hard against the rail: the servo will hum and get warm. Go back 2–3° from the point where it touches.

Wiring: brown/black = GND, red = 5 V (from a separate 5 V supply for several servos!), orange/yellow = signal to the pin.
