Input pin — where the button is wired

Choose the Arduino pin that the button, switch or sensor is connected to.

How to wire it: one wire of the button to the pin, the other wire to GND (minus, 0 V). That is all: no resistor needed, the board switches on its own internal “pull-up” resistor.

  pin D7 ───[ button ]─── GND
  • Do not connect a button to 5 V or 12 V: that can damage the Arduino.
  • Pin D2 is the DCC input and cannot be used.
  • A pin can only be used once: not by two inputs, and not by an input and an output.

Only DCC, no button? Choose “not used”.
