Pins — where the wires go

Choose the Arduino pin(s) for this output. Pin names are printed on the board: D3 … D13 and A0 … A5.

  • One pin can only be used once. A pin used twice turns red.
  • D2 is the DCC input. D0 / D1 are the USB connection; only some boards can use them (see the warning when you pick one).
  • ~PWM pins (D3, D5, D6, D9, D10, D11) can dim. Lighting effects need one of those. While any servo is in use, D9 and D10 can not dim.
  • On a DCCnext the list also shows the port number printed on that board.

An Arduino pin gives only a little current (max. 20 mA). Fine for one LED with a resistor or a servo signal wire. For relays, coils and lamps put a driver or relay module in between.
