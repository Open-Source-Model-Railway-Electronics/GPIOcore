Recorder — record and play back

The recorder lets the board remember what you do and do it again by itself, in a loop. Handy for a demonstration or exhibition layout: switch a few turnouts and signals once, and the board repeats it all day.

It needs one push button on the pin you choose here:

  • Hold the button for 1.5 seconds: recording starts. Now operate your layout as you want it. Every switching action is stored, with its timing. Hold the button again to stop recording.
  • Press the button briefly: playback starts and keeps repeating. Press briefly again: it finishes the current round and stops.

Only one input per board can be the recorder.

Tip: combine it with feedback inputs (track sensors), so playback waits for a train instead of using a fixed time.
