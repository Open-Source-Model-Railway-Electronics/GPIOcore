State and testing

Every output has a state: 0 or 1. What that means depends on the output: for a turnout 0 = curved and 1 = straight; for a light 0 = off and 1 = on.

Stored state: the board keeps the last state, also when the power is off. After switching on, the output goes back to it (coil turnouts excepted: they are not pulsed at power-up, so they do not all fire at once).

▶ Test: curved / off and ▶ Test: straight / on sends this output to the board straight away and switches it. Use this to check your wiring: does the right turnout move, in the right direction? If “straight” and “curved” are swapped, swap the two positions (servo) or the two wires / pins (coil motor).
