PWM: brightness and fading

A PWM output dims a lamp or LED string on a PWM pin (D3, D5, D6, D9, D10, D11): 0 = off, 255 = full brightness. Use a transistor or MOSFET module for more than about 20 mA.

  • Duty cycle — the brightness after power-up, until custom values, a route step or DCC extended set another one.
  • Fade time — how many seconds a full change from 0 to 255 takes; a half change takes half as long. at once (0) switches straight away. Every new brightness fades at this rate: from custom values, a route step or a DCC extended command. At power-up the output fades in from dark.

Example — day and night: an absolute input (a switch, or a DCC address) with Custom values off = 0 and on = 255, and a fade time of 60 s. Switching to night dims the lights in one minute; switching back brings the day in just as slowly.
