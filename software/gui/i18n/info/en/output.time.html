Switch time

How long the pin is switched on (pulse) or how fast it blinks (flashing). The board multiplies the number by the unit shown next to it, so you see the real time in milliseconds.

  • Coil turnout motors: 100–500 ms. Too short and the turnout does not move; too long and the coil gets hot. Start with 250 ms.
  • Flashing lights: 500 ms on / 500 ms off is a normal warning light.

Warning: never use “steady” for a coil turnout motor — the coil stays on and burns out.
