Input pin: where the button is wired

Choose the Arduino pin that the button, switch or sensor is connected to.

How to wire it: one wire of the button to the pin, the other wire to GND (minus, 0 V). No resistor needed: the board switches on its own internal "pull-up" resistor.

  pin D7 ---[ button ]--- GND
  • Do not connect a button to 5 V or 12 V: that can damage the Arduino.
  • D2 (DCC input), D13 (config LED) and A7 (config button) cannot be used.
  • A6 (Arduino Nano only) is an analog-only input without a pull-up: add a 10 kΩ resistor from the pin to 5 V.
  • Pins already used show red. Choosing one takes it from the other input or output, which becomes not in use.

Several buttons on one analog pin, or a sensor? Set Read as to an analog choice.

Only DCC, no button? Choose "not used".
