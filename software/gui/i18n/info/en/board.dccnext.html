Board type

  • Arduino Uno / Nano: pins D3–D12 and A0–A5. The Nano also has A6 (analog input only).
  • DCCnext (by ArCoMoRa): a ready-made board with an Arduino Nano, DCC input and screw terminals. The pin lists then also show the port numbers printed on the board.
  • Jack of all trades: our own board.
DCCnext port1–1011–16
Arduino pinD3–D12A0–A5

On every board D2 (DCC), D13 (config LED) and A7 (config button) are reserved. Dimming (PWM) works on D3, D5, D6, D9, D10 and D11 (DCCnext ports 1, 3, 4, 7, 8 and 9).
