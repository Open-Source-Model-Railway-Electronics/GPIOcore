DCCnext

The DCCnext (by ArCoMoRa) is a ready-made decoder board with an Arduino Nano, a DCC input with optocoupler and screw terminals. Tick this box if you use one: the program then shows the port numbers printed on the board next to the Arduino pin names.

Port1–1011–16
Arduino pinD3–D12A0–A5

D13 is not available on a DCCnext and turns red when you choose it. Dimming (PWM) works on ports 1, 3, 4 and 9.

Not sure what you have? A plain Arduino Nano on a breadboard or your own PCB: leave this unticked.
