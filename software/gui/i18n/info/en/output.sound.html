Sound effect: a DFPlayer Mini MP3 player

This output plays MP3 files from the micro SD card in a DFPlayer Mini module: a bell, a whistle, an announcement.

  D1 (TX) ---[1k]--- DFPlayer RX
  5 V -------------- DFPlayer VCC
  GND -------------- DFPlayer GND
  speaker (3 W max.) DFPlayer SPK_1 / SPK_2
  • The 1 kΩ resistor in the TX line protects the DFPlayer; nothing goes back to the Arduino.
  • Files on the SD card: 0001.mp3, 0002.mp3 … Track 1 is 0001.mp3.
  • Track when curved / off and Track when straight / on are played when the output switches. stop (0) stops playing.
  • A route step or a DCC extended command plays its own track number (0 = stop).
  • Several sound outputs may use D1 together, for example one per button, each with its own tracks.
  • Only without a PC: D1 is also the USB line to the computer. With the GUI connected the board only reports the track (♪ on the canvas). Unplug the USB cable and power the board otherwise to hear it.
