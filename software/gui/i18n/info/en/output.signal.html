4-aspect signal

A signal with up to four LEDs on pins A–D (for example green, yellow, red, white).

What the signal shows (the aspect) comes from your DCC command station (“signal” or “extended accessory” commands) or from a step in a route. The board remembers the last aspect, so after power-up the signal shows what it showed before.

Each LED needs a resistor (about 1 kΩ for 5 V). Connect the long leg (+) via the resistor to the pin and the short leg to GND — or the other way round for common-anode signals.
