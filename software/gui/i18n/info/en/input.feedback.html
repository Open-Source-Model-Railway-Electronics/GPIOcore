Feedback — a sensor the recorder waits for

A feedback input is a track sensor: a reed contact under the track with a magnet under the train, a light barrier, or a current detector. It tells the board: “a train is here”.

Why? Trains do not always take exactly the same time. When you record, the moment the sensor triggers is stored. When the recording is played back, the board stops at that point and waits until the sensor triggers again. So the train decides the timing, not a clock.

Feedback id: a number (0–15) to tell sensors apart. Give every sensor its own number.

Wire it like a button: sensor between the pin and GND.
