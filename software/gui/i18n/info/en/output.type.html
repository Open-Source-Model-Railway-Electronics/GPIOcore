What is connected to this output?

  • Single steady — one pin on or off. LEDs, a relay module, lamps (via a transistor or relay).
  • Single pulse — one pin gets a short pulse. For a decoupler or a single-coil device.
  • Single flashing — one pin blinks while switched on. A warning light.
  • Double steady — two pins: A on and B off, or the other way round. Two-colour signals, two relays.
  • Double pulse — two pins, one of them gets a short pulse. For coil turnout motors (Peco, Roco, Fleischmann …): A = one coil, B = the other coil.
  • Double flashing — two pins blink alternately.
  • Swing light — two lights take turns with a pause: level crossing lights.
  • Fireplace / TL tube / welder — lighting effects: flickering fire, a fluorescent tube that flickers on, welding flashes. (Fireplace and TL tube need PWM pins.)
  • 4-aspect signal — four LEDs (red, green, yellow, white …), set by DCC signal commands or by a route.
  • Servo — a model servo as turnout motor. Slow, realistic movement.
  • PWM — a fixed brightness for a lamp or LED string.
  • Not in use — nothing connected.

Important for coils: an Arduino pin cannot drive a coil directly. Use a driver board (for example a ULN2803 or a MOSFET module) between the pin and the coil.
