What is connected to this output?

  • Single steady — one pin on or off. LEDs, a relay module, lamps (via a transistor or relay).
  • Single pulse — one pin gets a short pulse. For a decoupler or a single-coil device.
  • Single flashing — one pin blinks while switched on. A warning light.
  • Double steady — two pins: A on and B off, or the other way round. Two-colour signals, two relays.
  • Double pulse — two pins, one of them gets a short pulse. For coil turnout motors (Peco, Roco, Fleischmann …): A = one coil, B = the other coil.
  • Double flashing — two pins blink alternately.
  • Light effect — choose the effect: swing light (two lights take turns with a pause: level crossing lights), fireplace, TL tube (a fluorescent tube that flickers on), welder (welding flashes) or photographer (a camera flash now and then). Fireplace and TL tube need PWM pins.
  • 4-aspect signal — four LEDs (green, yellow, red, white …), set by DCC signal commands or by a route.
  • Servo — a model servo as turnout motor. Slow, realistic movement.
  • PWM — a brightness for a lamp or LED string, optionally fading slowly (day and night).
  • Block signal — an automatic signal (lamps A–D): the board chooses the aspect itself from the detectors of its own block and the next block.
  • Sound effect — an MP3 player (DFPlayer Mini) on D1: a bell, a whistle, an announcement. The input chooses the track. Plays only when no PC is connected.
  • Board link (TX) — sends commands to other boards over TX (D1): each route step a DCC address with curved / straight or a value. Works only when no PC is connected.
  • Not in use — nothing connected.

Important for coils: an Arduino pin cannot drive a coil directly. Use a driver board (for example a ULN2803 or a MOSFET module) between the pin and the coil.
